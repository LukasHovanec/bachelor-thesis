===================================================================
INTERMODAL ROUTING ENGINE - TECHNICAL SUMMARY
===================================================================

1. DATASETS
- Air: US DOT Aviation Data (2024) + OpenFlights
- Rail: NJ Transit / Amtrak (2019) + OpenStreetMap (Nominatim)

2. GENERATED CSV FILES
- nodes_airports.csv  : 346 Airport nodes (airport_id,name,city,state,lat,lng,node_type)
- nodes_stations.csv  : 165 Station nodes (station_id,name,city,state,lat,lng,node_type)
- edges_flights.csv   : FLIGHT edges - regular lines (origin,dest,day_of_week,dep_time_min,arr_time_min,duration_min,hist_avg_delay_min,hist_delay_prob,flight_num,edge_type)
- edges_trains.csv    : TRAIN edges - regular lines (origin,dest,day_of_week,dep_time_min,arr_time_min,duration_min,hist_avg_delay_min,hist_delay_prob,train_id,edge_type)
- edges_transfers.csv : TRANSFER edges - connections between airports and stations (origin,dest,distance_km,duration_min,edge_type)

3. KEY LOGIC & PARAMETERS
- Schedule Times: Continuous minutes from midnight (0 - 1440 min)
- Transfer Radius: Max 50 km (Top-2 nearest station per airport)
- Transfer Asymmetry:
  * Station -> Airport (TRANSFER_TO_AIRPORT): Travel time + 150 min buffer
  * Airport -> Station (TRANSFER_TO_STATION): Travel time + 60 min buffer
- Delay Metrics: Average delay (min) + delay probability (>15 min)
